2025–PresentOngoing research · Platform developed

CounterLens: AI/ML Ethics Education

I study how interactive experiments can help students reason about model behavior, fairness, and trade-offs between evaluation criteria.

I designed and built the platform's frontend and backend so students can explore counterfactuals, adjust decision thresholds, and compare demographic groups without writing code. Classroom experiments are in preparation.

2026–PresentOngoing investigation · Evaluation planned

Investigating Contact-Induced Feature Mixing in 3D Shape Correspondence

I investigate whether spatial feature aggregation mixes distinct surfaces near self-contact and contributes to correspondence errors on 3D human shapes.

The planned evaluation compares aggregation methods while keeping input shapes, rendered views, and image features fixed, with attention to pose, visibility, and ground-truth validity.

2026–PresentProposed method · Evaluation planned

V-Audit: Visual Evidence Selection for Detecting Collateral Errors in GUI Agents

I investigate how selected before-and-after visual evidence can help people detect unintended edits made by GUI agents under a limited review budget.

The proposed evaluation examines missed errors, false rejection of correct outputs, review time, and confidence calibration.

2025Research project

Do Multimodal LLMs Understand Misleading Visualizations? Evaluating Visualization Literacy and Reasoning Errors in Chart Interpretation

I evaluated GPT-4 and Gemini 1.5 Pro on misleading charts, examining chart classification, trend interpretation, and critique.

The analysis considered quantitative performance and recurring reasoning errors involving visual distortions, missing context, and unsupported assumptions.
